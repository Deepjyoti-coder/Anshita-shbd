From The Heart

A Letter From Deep

D&A

Dear Anshita,

I don't think you realize how naturally you have become such a special part of my life.

Somewhere between our conversations, our random moments, and all the little things we share, you became someone I genuinely look forward to.

You're not just my good friend. You're my closest confidante and my bestie, someone I can laugh with, talk to, and make memories with in my own little way.

And honestly, that's what makes this connection so beautiful.

Sometimes I think about the first time I saw you in the faculty room. I remember looking into your eyes and getting completely lost for a moment. I had no idea that such an ordinary-looking moment would become something I would remember so fondly.

Then came 3 September, the day we talked for the very first time. It's funny how a simple conversation can become a date worth remembering. If someone had told me that day how special you would become to me, I probably wouldn't have believed them.

Even though we're both in Raipur, life doesn't always let us be where we want to be. Sometimes, 33 kilometers feel like much more when I wish I could simply spend some time with you.

But I love that we can still create moments together, even when we're busy with our own worlds.

Thank you for your smile, your care, your patience, your little ways of making things better, and for being the person you are.

And then there's our little number nine theory. Your birthday is on 25 October, mine is on 3 November, and there's a nine-day gap between them. Navratri gives us another little connection with its nine nights, and even the planets have become part of my excuse to flirt with you. Maybe these things are coincidences. But if they give me another reason to smile about us, I'm keeping the theory.

I hope this little website reminds you of something simple:

You matter to me.

Not just on your Birthday.

Not just when everything is perfect.

But in the ordinary moments, the random conversations, and the days when we don't even realize how much a small interaction can mean.

I don't need a grand occasion to appreciate you.

I just wanted to make something that feels a little like us.

Happy Birthday, Anshita. ❤️

Until our next little moment together,

Forever your Deep. ❤️
